        How to play

        1. Tap a tile, then tap a compound to put it there. You can drag too.
        2. A compound has one kind of + ion and one kind of − ion.
        3. The charges must add to zero: Ca²⁺ + 2 Cl⁻ → CaCl₂ is +2 and −2.
        4. Use the smallest ratio: CaO, not Ca₂O₂.
        5. Tap Lock. Enter the total + charge and total − charge (and the name, if names are on).
        6. Stuck? Put a tile in Swap to get 2 new tiles.
        7. Use every tile, then tap Done! Others check your compounds and flag mistakes.
        8. All right: you win. Any mistake: you're out, and the others keep going.

        Every correct locked compound earns ⚛. Catch a real mistake: +15. Flag a right one: −10.
